Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I submit a story tip?

Use the contact form above and select "Story Tip / Press Release" as the subject. For sensitive tips requiring confidentiality, please use Signal (number available upon request via email).

Are you available for speaking engagements?

Yes — I regularly speak at journalism schools, climate conferences, and press-freedom events. Select "Speaking Engagement" in the subject dropdown and provide event details including date, venue, and expected audience.

Can you collaborate on investigative projects?

Absolutely. I am open to cross-border investigative collaborations with journalists, NGOs, and research institutions working on accountability, environment, or human-rights stories.

What is your typical response time?

I personally read every message and aim to respond within 48 hours on working days (Sunday–Thursday in Bangladesh). Urgent press inquiries are given priority.

Do you accept freelance commissions?

On a selective basis. I accept commissions for longform investigative pieces, documentary consultations, and climate reporting from credible editorial outlets and NGOs.

How do I protect a sensitive tip?

Source safety is paramount. For highly sensitive information, please use encrypted channels. I follow CPJ and RSF protocols on source protection and never disclose source identities without explicit consent.
